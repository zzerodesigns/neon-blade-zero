# # Changelog: v48.8.7 to v48.8.8

## Third-Person Perspective System & Player Avatar Mesh
A third-person over-the-shoulder action camera mode has been introduced, accessible via a setting toggle in the system menu. The camera system operates by recording the player's physical eye position and orientation during physics resolution, then offsetting the view matrix backward by 2.8 units with a slight height and shoulder offset prior to rendering. Environmental collision raycasting is executed from the player's center of mass back toward the camera position, pulling the view forward when walls or obstacles intervene to prevent geometry clipping.

A dedicated player avatar mesh renders the player's presence in third-person view. The avatar features a custom GLSL shader material (`playerGlowMat`) configured with uniforms for time, theme index, and glow color. The fragment shader calculates local face coordinates and normal orientations to generate animated outer body edge outlines, scanlines, and Fresnel rim lighting. The shader automatically adapts its visual style to match each environmental theme, displaying frosted ice pulses in Light mode, stealth outlines in Dark mode, dual-tone digital scanlines in Neon mode, and flickering pumpkin ember waves in Halloween mode. Following the render pass, physical camera coordinates are restored to ensure total physics compatibility.

## Procedural Crescent Slash Geometry & Shader
The slash rendering system has been upgraded from basic 2D planar meshes to procedurally generated 3D crescent moon ribbon geometries. The new generator constructs arc vertices along a curved mathematical ribbon, tapering the width toward both tips via a sinusoidal function. Two distinct geometries are created at initialization: a wide sweeping crescent for standard slashes and a tighter crescent for precision click attacks.

The slash material has been converted to a custom shader material that computes smooth arc fading along the sweep curve, keeping the ribbon bright across eighty percent of its path before fading at the tips. Radial width falloff creates a glowing neon edge without hard geometric borders. In addition, a diverse slash angles setting has been added, allowing the attack arc to randomly pick from seven distinct slant angles including vertical, horizontal, diagonal, and backhand orientations. When third-person mode is active, slashes spawn at the player's physical location aligned with the crosshair vector.

## Experimental Traversal Maps
Two new experimental map types, `ARENA_EX` and `TITAN_EX`, have been added to the level generator. These maps introduce high-speed architectural traversal structures engineered to leverage the engine's OBB physics and wall-running mechanics.

The builder generates sloped inner vaulting rings surrounding the central arena, tilted at centrifugal bank angles to preserve slide momentum through turns. An ascending spiral parkour staircase climbs from ground level to high-altitude floating platforms, utilizing alternating sloped and flat pads calibrated to player jump trajectories. A dual-track parallel spiral wall-run structure wraps around the perimeter, composed of oriented bounding box wall panels generated with precise pitch, yaw, and roll angles to form a seamless surface for continuous high-speed wall-running.

## Player Death & Ragdoll Physics
The player death sequence and ragdoll simulation have been unified with enemy bot ragdoll physics. The player's ragdoll collision box bounds were corrected from double-height over-scaling down to an accurate 1.8-unit height centered at 0.9 units above the ground, eliminating subterranean clipping and infinite physics bounce loops upon impact.

The death simulation integrates free-fall gravity, 3D angular velocity, floor contact impulses, and ground drag. Once total kinetic velocity and spin fall below threshold limits, the ragdoll locks into a resting state flush with the floor. In first-person mode, the camera tracks the eye position of the tumbling avatar; in third-person mode, the camera orbits the ragdoll center of mass with smooth rotation controls intact. The total death duration has been extended to ten seconds, displaying an interactive breakout prompt ("PRESS ANY KEY TO CONTINUE") after five seconds that allows players to instantly trigger a respawn by pressing any key.

## Damage Logic, UI & Stability Adjustments
Several critical fixes were deployed across combat and user interface systems. The player damage calculation function was updated to verify that incoming damage originates from a melee slash before applying the Golden Buff instant kill multiplier, preventing non-blade sources such as grenade explosions from causing instant death.

Explosive damage from projectiles now applies non-fire blast damage, allowing grenade explosions to kill the player even when passive Blaze is active, while preserving immunity against pure burn status ticks and direct fire contact. UI backdrop blur rendering was stabilized by adding clip-path updates whenever the Echo score panel changes visibility or value. Dead bot corpses now remain on the field for five seconds before fading out, and burn status timers are automatically synchronized with the corpse timer so burning embers persist until the body disappears. Finally, the settings menu was reorganized with a collapsible drawer for secondary joke toggles.
